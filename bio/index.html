Born in 1993 in Porto, Portugal, holding a pencil in her hand straight out of the womb, Sara started drawing everywhere she should not have, compulsively. The hospital walls, the birth certificate, the nurse's face. Eventually calmed down when the frontal lobe kicked in, and studied Fine Arts and Design. Currently works as a Product Designer, not only because it pays the bills but because she actually likes it. Whenever the corporate job doesn't knock her out, she likes to produce drawings that she sometimes calls illustrations and other times comics, because she is not sure of the difference. She absolutely wrote this herself in the third person.
