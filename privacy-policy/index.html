Privacy policy

Last updated 21 Aug 2026

The short version

This site doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you, and doesn't run any analytics or ads. Everything this site needs to run (the code, the fonts, the icons, all of it) is self-hosted. Nothing loads from third-party servers, so no outside company sees you just for visiting. The only personal data I ever collect is your email address, and only if you choose to sign up for the newsletter. Everything below just explains that in more detail.

Where this site lives

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. That means when you visit, GitHub's servers do the actual work of sending the pages to your browser, and like any web host, GitHub can log basic technical information to keep things running and secure, including IP addresses. This happens for every website on the internet: a server can't send a page to your browser without knowing where to send it. I don't have access to those server logs myself, that's GitHub's side of things, used for security and to keep the service working. If you want the details on what they collect, GitHub's privacy statement covers it.

The newsletter

If you sign up for the newsletter, I collect one thing: your email address. That's it. I use it for exactly one purpose: sending you the newsletter. Nothing else, and I'll never sell it or hand it to anyone for advertising. The legal basis for this is your consent: you give it by entering your email and subscribing. You can take it back whenever you like.

The emails are sent through Buttondown, an email service that handles the sending and stores the subscriber list on my behalf. Buttondown is based in the US, so your email address is stored there. That transfer is covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (the standard legal mechanism for sending data outside the EU), and Buttondown only ever processes your email to provide the service, they don't collect anything beyond what you give them. You can read Buttondown's privacy policy and data processing agreement if you want the full picture.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email. You can also ask for a copy of your data or ask me to delete it entirely, and that gets handled right away too. I keep your email on the list until you unsubscribe or ask me to remove it, and no longer. And if you ever feel I've mishandled your data, you have the right to complain to your data protection authority. In Portugal, that's the CNPD (Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados).
